## Tax note (internal — not part of the customer terms)

California's treatment of SaaS and digital products changes on **2027-01-01**.
If services are sold to California customers, tax obligations may follow the
purchaser's California address. Using a **Merchant of Record** makes the MoR the
seller of record and shifts the collection liability to it; that is the
recommended route and is an owner/counsel decision, not one to be assumed here.
